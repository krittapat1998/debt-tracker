## App Privacy

Data collection: **"No, we do not collect data from this app."** The app has no
server, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs that send data. Crash reports that
users opt into sharing through Apple don't count as collection.

## Export compliance

Same answers as every TestFlight build: standard encryption algorithms (option 2),
not distributed in France. The only encryption is AES-256-GCM for password-protected
backup files (Dart `cryptography`).

**Notes** (English, for the reviewer)

```
Debt Tracker is a personal tracker for the user's own credit cards, cash cards and personal loans. All data stays on the device: there is no account, login or server, so no demo account is needed. The interface is in Thai.

How to try it:
1. On the home tab (ภาพรวม), tap "เพิ่มบัตร" (Add card, top right). Enter a name, an outstanding balance, an interest rate and a due day, then save.
2. The home tab now shows the total debt, the amount due this month and which debt to pay first.
3. Tap "วางแผนปลดหนี้" (Debt-free plan) and type a monthly amount such as 25000 to see when every debt is paid off.
4. Other tabs: ปฏิทิน (calendar of due dates), ประวัติ (history), คำนวณโปะ (prepayment calculator), ตั้งค่า (settings: theme, reminders, backup, Face ID lock, privacy policy).

Notes:
- The app does not lend money, connect to banks or give financial advice. Figures are estimates to help users plan.
- Bank SMS import on iPhone only reads text the user pastes in ("นำเข้า SMS"); nothing is sent anywhere.
- Face ID only locks the app. Notifications are local reminders.
- Backups can be encrypted with a user password (AES-256-GCM), declared in export compliance.
```
